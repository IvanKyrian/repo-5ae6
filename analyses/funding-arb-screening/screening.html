Largest peak-to-trough drop in cumulative funding. P&L assumes we held today's notional through that period. Funding only, no M2M.

Exposure: current size and open interest

Portfolio AuM . Margin = both legs at 5×. Headroom = distance to 5% of the smaller venue's OI (the limit agreed for oil, shown for all four).

Methodology

  1. Data. Settlement-level funding for each perp: Binance every 8h (XMR, ZEC) or 4h (BZ, CL), Hyperliquid every 1h. The files give annualized rates, so each settlement is converted to what was actually paid (rate × hours ÷ 8,760) and summed by day.
  2. Trade. Long Binance, short Hyperliquid on all four. Hyperliquid has paid more than Binance on average in every arb, and in every month since May. Daily spread = Hyperliquid funding − Binance funding, as % of notional per leg.
  3. Windows. 7d, 30d and 90d are the average daily spread over the last 7, 30 and 90 days to 24 Nov, × 365. Inception starts on the first full day both venues have data. For oil, April is excluded, so oil starts 1 May.
  4. Implied APR. Spread × 2.5. At 5×, $1 of notional per leg needs $0.20 of margin on each venue, so $0.40 in total.
  5. Worst drawdown. Largest peak-to-trough drop in cumulative spread. The period runs from the day after the peak to the trough. Recovery counts the days from the trough until cumulative funding is back at the peak.
  6. Exposure. Notional, OI and AuM come from the positions sheet. Drawdown P&L = drawdown % × current notional per leg.

Checks and caveats

  • No missing days, and every day has the expected number of settlements on both venues. The 7d, 30d and 90d spreads match the moving averages in the source files, e.g. Brent 90d: −38.4% − (−44.7%) = 6.3%.
  • XMR legs don't match: $1.14M on Binance vs $1.59M on Hyperliquid. The table uses the hedged $1.14M. At $1.59M the worst-drawdown P&L would be about −$6.1k.
  • WTI labels in the positions sheet look swapped. CL is Binance's ticker but is listed under Hyperliquid. The table takes the CL row as Binance and the WTI row as Hyperliquid. Notional is the same on both legs, so only the OI split depends on this.
  • About 45% of Binance oil settlements are exactly 0%, mostly on weekends. They are real settlements and are included.
  • HYPE isn't included because there was no data for it in this batch.
