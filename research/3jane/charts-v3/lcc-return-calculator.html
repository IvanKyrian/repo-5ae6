Public LCC: uncalled, the margin earns Aave + a 1.25% fee on the full commitment (= 16.7% on margin). Called, we hold USD3 (+0.75% for the first 35 days). Trenches deal: uncalled for up to 4 call windows (~90 days), the margin earns Aave + 12% USD + 18% in JANE; after that it rolls into public terms. If called within the term, the USD3 earns a fixed 18% for 70 days (USD3's own yield plus a JANE top-up), then USD3's normal yield. Margin is returned when called. No losses, gas or fees assumed.
